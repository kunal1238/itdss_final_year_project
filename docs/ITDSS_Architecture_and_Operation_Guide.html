Intelligent Trading Decision Support System — Architecture, Mathematical Specifications & Run Manual (v2.0.0)
IMPORTANT NOTICE: Educational Research Platform, Not Financial Advice
ITDSS is an explainable quantitative decision-support tool. It places no live orders and connects to no broker execution APIs. In rigorous purged walk-forward backtesting across 37 instruments, the current model has no statistically proven predictive edge over a no-skill baseline (such as majority-class prediction or passive buy-and-hold). The platform explicitly surfaces this finding on every analysis page and model card.

1. System Overview & Core Principles

The Intelligent Trading Decision Support System (ITDSS) provides risk-first, explainable algorithmic analysis for 37 global instruments spanning 5 asset classes (Forex, Cryptocurrencies, Equity Indices, Commodities, and Stocks), alongside arbitrary ticker queries from Yahoo Finance.

  • Explainability over Black Boxes: Every trade signal includes feature contribution attributions (Saabas tree path decomposition), showing precisely why a decision was reached.
  • Strict Triple Barrier Risk Geometry: Stop loss fixed at -1.5 ATR, take profit at +3.0 ATR (2:1 Reward-to-Risk ratio), and a 24-bar maximum time horizon.
  • Scale-Free Machine Learning: Technical indicators are normalized into dimensionless ratios (ATR fractions or percentage-of-range), allowing one single model to score assets with wildly divergent nominal price levels.
  • Full Accountability Journal: Live recommendations are timestamped and committed to an append-only SQLite database upon issuance, and settled as subsequent price candles unfold.
  • No Repainting / No Lookahead: The pipeline strictly evaluates closed price candles, completely discarding the active forming bar.

2. High-Level Architecture

ITDSS Core System Pipeline

1. Data Layer (data_fetcher.py)

Fetches Yahoo Finance bars, sanitizes UTC timestamps, enforces closed-bar cuts, converts minor currencies (GBp → GBP), and caches responses in a single-flight LRU store.

2. Feature Engine (indicators.py)

Calculates 9 scale-free ratios: Williams %R, RSI, Relative ATR, Relative Volatility, 50/200 EMA Spread, EMA Slope, Distance from EMA, and 10/50 Momentum.

3. ML Scorer (ml_scorer.py & training.py)

Random Forest (300 trees) with Platt Sigmoid Probability Calibration. Computes positive expectancy filter, TradeScore ranking, and Saabas feature drivers.

4. Risk & Position Engine (risk.py)

Sizes trades dynamically based on account capital, user risk %, FX conversion, ML confidence multiplier (0.5 to 1.0), and enforces ESMA regulatory leverage caps.

5. Signal Journal (journal.py)

Append-only SQLite tracking. Live daemon thread verifies price evolution, settling open signals to win, loss, or timeout, and updates empirical performance.

6. Delivery Layer (FastAPI & React 19)

RESTful backend serving JSON endpoints and static bundled frontend. React UI supports 6 languages (including RTL Arabic), dark/light modes, and colorblind safety.

3. Scale-Free Feature Engineering

Traditional technical indicators depend heavily on nominal price levels. In ITDSS, all indicators are transformed into dimensionless ratios:

Feature Mathematical Formulation Analytical Role
williams_r -100 × (Max14(H) - C) / (Max14(H) - Min14(L) + ε) Overbought/oversold oscillator bounded in [-100, 0].
rsi 100 × EMA14(Up) / (EMA14(Up) + EMA14(Down) + ε) Wilder-smoothed momentum velocity bounded in [0, 100].
atr_rel ATR14 / (SMA200(ATR14) + ε) Current market volatility relative to its long-term norm.
vol_rel σ20(returns) / (σ100(returns) + ε) Short-term volatility compression vs medium-term volatility.
ema_spread (EMA50(C) - EMA200(C)) / ATR14 Macro trend strength expressed in volatility units (ATR).
ema_slope (EMA50(C)t - EMA50(C)t-10) / ATR14 Velocity and curvature of the primary trend filter.
dist_fast (C - EMA50(C)) / ATR14 Mean-reversion displacement from fast moving average.
mom_10 (Ct - Ct-10) / ATR14 10-period directional price momentum in ATR units.
mom_50 (Ct - Ct-50) / ATR14 50-period intermediate price momentum in ATR units.

4. Mathematical Modeling & Triple Barrier Labeling

4.1 Triple Barrier Construction

Signals are evaluated with asymmetric price barriers and a hard time horizon:

  • Target (Take Profit): Entry + 3.0 × ATR for BUY (Entry - 3.0 × ATR for SELL).
  • Stop Loss: Entry - 1.5 × ATR for BUY (Entry + 1.5 × ATR for SELL).
  • Horizon Timeout: 24 bars. If neither barrier is hit by bar 24, trade closes at the 24th bar Close.
  • Conservative Execution Rules: If a bar touches both Stop and Target, the Stop loss takes precedence. Gaps beyond the stop fill at the open price.

4.2 Expectancy-Based Decision Rule

The system emits a BUY or SELL signal only if expected return after costs is strictly positive:

E[R] = Pwin × RR + (1 - Pwin) × R̄nonwin - CostR > 0

Where RR = 2.0, R̄nonwin is the empirical historical average outcome of non-winning trades, and CostR converts asset-class spread/slippage into R units. If E[R] ≤ 0, the trade is marked NEUTRAL.

4.3 TradeScore Ranking

Active opportunities across the 37 instruments are prioritized via TradeScore:

TradeScore = (Pwin × RR) / atr_rel

This scores high-probability setups higher, while penalizing instruments experiencing extreme, unstable volatility spikes.

5. Dynamic Risk & Position Sizing

Positions are sized so that a hit on the stop loss incurs a loss equal to exactly the user-specified percentage of capital, converted into the user's account currency:

Risk per Unit = |Entry - Stop| × FXinstrument → account

Msize = clip(0.5 + 5 × (Pwin - Pbreakeven), 0.5, 1.0)

Position Units = (Capital × Risk% × Msize) / Risk per Unit
Regulatory Leverage Protection: Position sizes are capped automatically by ESMA-modeled institutional limits: Forex (30x), Indices (20x), Commodities (10x), Stocks (5x), Cryptocurrencies (2x).
